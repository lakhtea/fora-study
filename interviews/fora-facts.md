# Fora, in facts you can use (sources: foratravel.com/careers/engineering, the posting, TechCrunch and Forbes July 2026, Built In, Glassdoor)

- Founded 2021 in New York by Henley Vazquez, Jake Peters (co-founder, CTO and CPO), and Evan Frank. Tribeca HQ. 250+ employees. In office Monday, Tuesday, Thursday.
- July 16, 2026: $60M Series D at a $1B valuation (Forerunner and Tactile Ventures, with Thrive, Insight, Heartcore, PLUS, BlackPines, Tribeca VP). $138.5M raised. Part funds Via, the AI assistant for advisors.
- 15,000+ active advisors; $3B+ booked; over 1 million hotel bookings; advisors over $2M per year tripled year over year. TIME100 Most Influential Companies 2026.
- Stack signals: Next.js, TypeScript, React, Node.js on the front; Django (Python) core platform; AWS, GCP, Kubernetes, BigQuery; Contentful, Cloudinary, Sentry on the marketing site. The advisor portal's exact framework is unconfirmed.
- Pods: Advisor Enablement (itinerary builder with AI writing assistant, client tools, mobile), Booking Platform (supplier search, hotel integrations beyond legacy GDS, room mapping, rate parity), Membership and Growth (signup, onboarding, training, settings), Finance and Revenue (ledger, commissions, finance integrations), Web and Growth (consumer site, advisors' public presence).
- Building now: multi-agent AI (voice prompts, next-action prediction); a booking platform connecting to hotel PMS, expanding to cruise, car, activities, insurance; traveler co-creation in real time; commission infrastructure targeting 99%+ accuracy across 90+ countries; Price Drop ($1.6M saved), Bookable Quote ($17M GMV in two months), Deals.
- How they work: high autonomy and accountability; craft (eng specs reviewed across pods, thorough code review, factual post-mortems); ruthless focus on impact; no territories or bad ideas (engineers suggest requirement changes, PMs vibe-code drafts); no deadlines, only timelines; public career levels, same pay at the same level; engineering initiatives and hackathons; AI integrated into CI/CD and standard dev practice.
- People named publicly: Jake Peters (CTO/CPO), Sam Gohh (EM), Dan Milisits (Senior Frontend Engineer), Kyle Rose and Paul Banel (Senior SWEs).
- The posting: build the advisor application (onboarding, searching and booking, itineraries, client interactions); partner with PMs and designers; define end-to-end solutions with backend engineers; 5+ years frontend, 3+ React; $185K to $235K plus equity, one standardized figure per level, level determined by performance across the interview process. It lists a CS bachelor's; you passed the screen without one; one sentence if asked (App Academy, then five years shipping platform work at LTV and Bank of America).
- The loop: (1) React debugging, 45 minutes in CoderPad, 3 bugs; (2) Technical Assessment, 2 hours on Zoom: API Design 60 then React Intensive 60; (3) Technical Leadership on Zoom: EM 60 plus social call 30; (4) onsite final, 90 minutes: engineering leadership team plus coffee with the team.
